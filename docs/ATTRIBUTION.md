## English

**Software and documentation:** Yakub Uyar developed the application. This includes the React/Next.js components, API, database and integration of the Rive files and their state controls.

**Rive animations:** The files under `public/rive/` are an anonymous design contribution. According to Yakub Uyar, their author permitted their use and publication in this project. This permission does not transfer authorship or automatically place the files under the original software license. Independent reuse of these materials requires clarification of the applicable rights. The actual development contribution is preserved in the Git history with anonymous attribution.

**Hero illustration:** The illustration on the start page is located at `public/images/hero.jpeg`.

**Video and audio:** The video at `public/videos/generated_video_mp.mp4` and the audio at `public/sounds/text_sound.mp3` were created by Yakub Uyar.

**Screenshots and sample data:** Captures under `assets/screenshots/` were taken from the running application. The questionnaire runs and community posts provided in `prisma/seed.js` are fictional sample data, not real testimonials or user numbers.

**Libraries and fonts:** Dependencies are listed in `package.json` and `package-lock.json`. Their own licenses and notices remain applicable. The application uses Inter, Instrument Serif and JetBrains Mono through `next/font/google`; these fonts are provided under the SIL Open Font License. Running and building the application may require Rive runtime files or font files from external sources.

**Content context:** The questionnaire, tips and charts are illustrative. They do not provide a scientific or medical diagnosis. The chart values are not supported by verifiable individual sources and are therefore labelled as illustrative content.
